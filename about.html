Our Story

Less noise.
More character.

A restaurant identity built on ingredient-led food, thoughtful design and confident simplicity.

The Philosophy

Food that feels considered, not complicated.

Salt & Sage brings a modern point of view to everyday dining. The menu is focused, the ingredients are seasonal, and the room is designed to feel warm rather than formal.

The visual identity uses soft stone tones, sage greens and restrained typography to mirror the food: natural, elegant and confident.

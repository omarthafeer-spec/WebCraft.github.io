The Idea

Designed around the social side of the place.

The Couch is not positioned like a traditional café. The website makes experiences the product: celebrate, sing, create, meet and hang out.

The visual direction mixes warm terracotta, soft neutrals and playful typography so it feels youthful without becoming childish.

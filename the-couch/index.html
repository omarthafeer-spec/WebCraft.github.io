The Idea

Social, comfortable and easy to come back to.

The Couch is positioned as a place where coffee is only part of the experience. The website therefore gives equal weight to the space, the activities and the community.

The visual direction uses warm terracotta, soft cream and muted sage to feel playful and welcoming without becoming childish.
